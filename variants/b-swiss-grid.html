Why Cub Scouts

Character

Scouts grow up trustworthy, kind, helpful, brave. A three-year Tufts study proved it.

Outdoors

Hike. Camp. Build. Explore. Real adventure, every month of the year.

Family

You and your scout. Together. Lifelong memories.

How Scouting Works

Den
Your scout's grade group, led by a den leader. Dens meet once or twice a month and earn rank together.
Pack
Pack 57 brings all six dens together — across grades, scouts and families. One pack meeting and one pack activity per month.
Council
Pacific Skyline Council supports packs across the peninsula — training leaders, running camps, handling registration.

Our Pack Leadership

Cubmaster
Runs the pack. Leads pack meetings, sets the year's direction.
Den Leaders
One or two parents per den. Organize monthly den meetings, guide rank advancement.
Committee
Treasurer, registrar, event chairs — parents who keep the pack running.

What makes Pack 57 different

Pack 57 is chartered by its own parents. We build it, run it, and hand it down. That's why we're one of the longest-running packs in the nation.

What we ask of you

Pack 57 is a family pack. Every family pitches in — drive to a campout, lead a den meeting, run a station at the derby, cook a meal at blue & gold.

The more you bring, the more your scout gets back.
