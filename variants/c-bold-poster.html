Pack 57 is a family Cub Scout pack. We hike, build, camp, and earn — alongside our kids, not from the sidelines.

What is a Pack?

A pack is the local Cub Scout unit — that's us, Pack 57. Inside the pack, scouts are grouped by grade into dens that meet through the school year for adventure and rank-earning.

What is a Council?

A council is the regional Scouting America organization that supports many packs at once — training leaders, running summer camps, and keeping the program safe and consistent.
