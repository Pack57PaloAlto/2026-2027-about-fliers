What is a Pack?

Your Pack is the local Cub Scout group — Pack 57 right here in Palo Alto. Inside the pack, scouts are grouped by grade into dens. We meet, camp, and adventure together.

What is a Council?

A Council is the regional organization that supports packs across many cities — training leaders, running camps, and keeping the program safe and consistent.
